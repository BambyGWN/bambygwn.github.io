Tempo Home - Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Tempo Home (Wick) is a private home assistant used only by its owner, on a single device in the owner's home. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own Gmail messages and Google Calendar events, and can create email drafts and calendar events that the owner confirms on screen.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show the owner their own email and calendar and to answer the owner's questions.
  • Data stays on the owner's device. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Short text excerpts may be sent to an AI service to generate answers for the owner; they are not stored there for other purposes.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account settings, and can delete all stored data on the device.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Oura Ring data

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own Oura Ring data: daily sleep, activity (including steps and calories) and readiness summaries, heart rate and heart-rate variability, stress and resilience, blood oxygen, workouts, sessions, tags, and ring information such as battery level. It also reads basic account information so it can tell which Oura account the data belongs to.

  • This data is read from the Oura API into the owner's own Home Assistant instance, running on hardware in the owner's home.
  • It is used only to show the owner their own health and activity data and to answer the owner's questions about it.
  • It is stored only on the owner's device. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time in their Oura account settings, and can delete the stored data on the device.
